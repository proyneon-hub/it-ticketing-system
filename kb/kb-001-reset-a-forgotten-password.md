# Reset a forgotten password

Most password resets can be done without contacting the service desk.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your work email address and approve the verification prompt sent to your registered MFA device.
3. Choose a new password that meets the rules shown on the page.
4. Sign in on your laptop with the new password.
5. Update the saved password on your phone (mail, calendar, Wi-Fi). An old password saved on a phone keeps retrying and will lock the account again.

## If it does not work

- No verification prompt arrives: see KB-004.
- You no longer have your registered MFA device: see KB-003.
- The page says the account is locked: see KB-002.
- The service desk never asks for your password. If anyone does, treat it as phishing and see KB-029.
