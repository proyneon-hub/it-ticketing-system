# What to include in a ticket when something is broken

A ticket that says only "it is broken" cannot be worked on until someone asks questions. Answering these first gets you a faster fix.

## Include

1. **What you were trying to do,** for example "print a report" or "join a Teams call".
2. **What happened instead,** including the exact wording of any error message. A screenshot is ideal.
3. **When it started,** and whether it ever worked.
4. **What you have already tried,** such as restarting.
5. **Which device and where,** for example "my work laptop, at home on Wi-Fi", and the laptop's asset number if you know it.
6. **Who else is affected.** If several people have the same problem, say so.
7. **How urgent it is,** and why: for example a client meeting at 3pm.

## Report one problem per ticket

Two unrelated problems in one ticket are harder to track and can be slowed down by each other. Raise them separately.

## Common first steps

Restarting the device, and checking you are on the right network or the VPN, solve many problems. See KB-008 for Wi-Fi and KB-007 for the VPN.
