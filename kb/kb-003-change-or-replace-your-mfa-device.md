# Change or replace your MFA device

Use this when you have a new phone, or the old one is lost, broken or reset.

## If you still have the old phone

1. Keep the old phone until you finish, and sign in on your laptop.
2. Open your security settings and choose **Add sign-in method**.
3. Follow the prompts to install the authenticator app on the new phone and scan the QR code.
4. Approve one test sign-in on the new phone.
5. Remove the old phone from your list of sign-in methods.

## If you no longer have the old phone

You cannot add a new device yourself, because the old one is what proves it is you. Raise a ticket from a colleague's device or by phone. The service desk will verify your identity before a technician resets your MFA registration. Have your manager's name and your employee number ready.

If the phone was lost or stolen, also see KB-030.
