# A web application loads wrongly or shows old data

A company web page looks broken, will not load, keeps sending you back to sign in, or shows data that is out of date.

## Steps

1. Refresh the page with Ctrl and F5 on Windows, or Command, Shift and R on a Mac. This bypasses the stored copy of the page.
2. Open the same page in a private or incognito window. If it works there, the cause is stored browser data or an extension.
3. Clear the cache and cookies for that site only. In the browser, open the site's information from the padlock in the address bar and choose to clear its cookies and site data. Sign in again afterwards.
4. Turn off browser extensions one by one, especially ad blockers and password managers, and test again.
5. Try a different browser. If the page works, tell the service desk which browser fails.
6. Restart the browser after installing any pending update.

## What to put in the ticket

The page address, the browser and version, what you see, and the exact time. A screenshot helps. If the same page fails for a colleague, say so, because it then points at the application and not your browser.
