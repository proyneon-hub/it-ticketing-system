# Software licence expired or not recognised

A program says the licence has expired, is missing, or that you are not licensed to use it.

## Steps

1. Close the program completely and open it again.
2. Sign out of the program, if it has an account menu, and sign back in with your work email. A licence is tied to that account, and signing in with a personal address shows as unlicensed.
3. Check your internet connection. Many programs check the licence online and show an error when they cannot reach the licence server.
4. Restart the laptop and try again.
5. Check the company software portal for an update. Old versions sometimes stop accepting a renewed licence.

## When to raise a ticket

If it still shows as unlicensed, raise a ticket with:

- the program name and version, and the exact wording of the message
- the email address you signed in with
- the date the licence was meant to run to, if you know it

Licences are renewed and assigned by the software owner, so the service desk may need approval from your manager. Design suites and similar programs are often renewed once a year, so an expiry that affects many people at once is worth reporting straight away.
