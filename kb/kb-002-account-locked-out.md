# Account locked after too many sign-in attempts

An account locks automatically after several wrong passwords in a row. It unlocks by itself after 30 minutes, or sooner if you reset the password.

## Steps

1. Wait 30 minutes without trying to sign in on any device. Every failed attempt restarts the wait.
2. While you wait, sign out of the mail app and any other app on your phone, and turn off its Wi-Fi for a minute. A phone that still holds an old password is the most common cause of repeated lockouts.
3. After 30 minutes, sign in once on your laptop with the correct password.
4. If you are not sure of the password, use the reset steps in KB-001 instead of guessing.
5. Update the password on your phone straight away.

## When to raise a ticket

- The account locks again within the hour: a device or a script is still using the old password.
- The sign-in history shows attempts you did not make. Treat this as a possible compromise and see KB-030.
