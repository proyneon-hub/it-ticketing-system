# Monitor flickers or shows no signal

The screen flickers, goes black after the laptop wakes from sleep, or says **No signal**.

## Steps

1. Check the monitor is switched on and set to the right input (HDMI, DisplayPort or USB-C). Press the input button on the monitor to cycle through them.
2. Unplug the video cable at both ends and plug it back in firmly. Try a different cable if you have one.
3. If you use a docking station, unplug its power for 30 seconds, plug it back in, then reconnect the laptop.
4. Wake the laptop by pressing a key, then close and reopen the lid.
5. On Windows, press the Windows key, Ctrl, Shift and B together to reset the graphics driver. On a Mac, unplug and reconnect the display.
6. Set the refresh rate back to 60 Hz in the display settings, because a higher setting often causes flicker on older cables.
7. Restart the laptop with the monitor connected.

## If it keeps happening

Raise a ticket with the monitor model, the dock model if any, and the type of cable. Say whether it also happens on another monitor.
