# Teams calls have no audio or camera

You cannot hear others, they cannot hear you, or your camera shows a black picture.

## Steps

1. In the call, open the device settings and check the right speaker, microphone and camera are selected. A headset that was unplugged earlier often leaves Teams pointing at the wrong device.
2. Check the microphone is not muted, both in Teams and with the mute button on the headset or keyboard.
3. Check the volume is up and the headset is not paired to your phone. A Bluetooth headset can connect to the phone instead.
4. Close every other app that may hold the camera or microphone, such as another video call or a recording tool.
5. On Windows open Settings, Privacy and security, and on a Mac open System Settings, Privacy and security. Allow Teams to use the microphone and camera.
6. Quit Teams completely from the taskbar or menu bar and start it again, then join a test call.
7. Restart the laptop if it still fails.

## If it still fails

Raise a ticket saying whether it fails with every headset or just one, and whether the built-in speaker works. Ask a colleague whether they can hear you on a plain phone call, to tell whether the fault is Teams or the hardware.
