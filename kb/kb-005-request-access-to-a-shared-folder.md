# Request access to a shared folder or drive

Access to shared folders is granted by the folder's owner, not by the service desk. The service desk records the request and passes it to Access Management once the owner has approved.

## What to include in your ticket

1. The full path or link of the folder, exactly as you see it.
2. What you need: read only, or read and edit.
3. Why you need it, in one sentence.
4. The name of the folder owner or your manager, if you know it.

## What happens next

1. Access Management asks the folder owner to approve. Requests without an owner's approval are not actioned.
2. Once approved, access is added to your account. It normally appears within an hour.
3. Sign out and back in, or restart your computer, to pick up the new access.

## Before you ask

If you could open the folder yesterday and cannot today, that is probably a fault, not a missing permission. Try KB-010 first and mention it in the ticket.
