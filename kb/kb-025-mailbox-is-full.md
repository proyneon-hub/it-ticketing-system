# Mailbox is full or almost full

You see a warning that the mailbox is full, or new mail is bounced back to senders.

## Steps

1. Empty **Deleted Items** and **Junk Email**, because both still count towards your limit.
2. Sort the mailbox by size. In Outlook choose Sort, then Size, and look at the largest messages first.
3. Save large attachments to your shared drive or OneDrive, then delete the emails that carried them.
4. Delete old newsletters and automatic notifications you no longer read.
5. Check **Sent Items**, where large attachments you sent are also stored.
6. Wait a few minutes after deleting. The warning clears once the mailbox goes below the limit.

## Keeping it small

Set up a rule to move newsletters to a folder, and turn on automatic archiving if your organisation allows it.

## When to raise a ticket

If the mailbox is still full after clearing it, or you need to keep the mail for legal or work reasons, raise a ticket. A technician can check the limit and, with your manager's approval, raise it or set up an archive. Do not delete mail you are required to keep.
