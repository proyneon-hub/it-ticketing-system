# Printer shows offline

The computer lists the printer as offline or unavailable, and nothing prints.

## Steps

1. Look at the printer itself. Check it is on, has no error on its screen, and is not out of paper or toner.
2. Check the printer's network connection: a cable plugged in, or Wi-Fi shown on its screen. Restart the printer and wait two minutes.
3. Make sure your laptop is on the office network, or the VPN if you are remote. Office printers cannot be reached from a plain home connection.
4. On Windows, open Settings, Printers and scanners, choose the printer, open **Printer properties**, and make sure **Use printer offline** is not ticked. On a Mac, remove the printer and add it again as described in KB-013.
5. Restart the laptop and print a test page.

## If it still shows offline

If the queue is stuck with jobs waiting, see KB-012. If nobody in the area can print, raise one ticket with the printer's name (it is on a label on the front) and where it is.
