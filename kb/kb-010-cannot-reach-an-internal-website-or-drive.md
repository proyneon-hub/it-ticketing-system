# Cannot reach an internal website or shared drive

An internal site will not load, or a shared drive that worked before is missing or asks for a password.

## Steps

1. Check you are on the office network or connected to the VPN. Internal sites and drives are not reachable from the plain internet. If you are at home, connect the VPN first (see KB-007 if it will not connect).
2. Open a public website to confirm your internet works.
3. For a website: try the address in a private browser window. If it works there, clear the browser's cache and cookies for that site.
4. For a shared drive: disconnect the drive, then reconnect it by typing its path into the file manager's address bar.
5. If a password prompt appears for the drive, sign out and back in to your account, then try again.
6. Restart the laptop.

## What to put in the ticket

The exact address or drive path, the exact message, whether you are on the VPN, and whether a colleague can reach it. If you have never had access, see KB-005.
