# Calendar invites or email missing on my phone

Meetings and mail show on your laptop but not on your phone, or arrive late.

## Steps

1. Check the phone has a connection and that Low Data Mode or battery saver is not stopping background updates.
2. Open the mail or calendar app and pull down to refresh.
3. In the app's settings, check that **Calendar** and **Mail** are both switched on for your work account.
4. Check the sync period. Mail apps often sync only the last few days or weeks, so older items look missing.
5. If the account shows a warning or a password prompt, sign in again. See KB-024 for the same problem on a laptop, and KB-001 if the password has changed recently.
6. As a last step, remove the work account from the phone and add it again. You will need to approve MFA, and the mail and calendar download again, which takes a few minutes.

## When to raise a ticket

If it still fails, raise a ticket with the phone model, the operating system version and the app used. If invites are missing on the laptop as well, say so, because that points at the mailbox and not the phone.
