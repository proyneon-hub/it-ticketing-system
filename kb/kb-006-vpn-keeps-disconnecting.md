# VPN keeps disconnecting

The VPN connects, then drops every few minutes, often when you are on a call or moving a large file.

## Steps

1. Check your home internet first: open a public website without the VPN. If it is slow or drops, restart your router and try again.
2. If you are on Wi-Fi, move closer to the router or connect a cable. A weak signal is the most common cause.
3. Fully disconnect the VPN, close the client, and reconnect.
4. Turn off any second VPN or security tool running on the laptop. Two of them fighting over the network causes drops.
5. Restart the laptop, then connect the VPN before opening other apps.
6. In the VPN client settings, switch the connection type or protocol if the client offers a choice, and reconnect.

## If it still drops

Raise a ticket with the time of the last three drops, whether you were on Wi-Fi or a cable, and the message the client showed. If the VPN will not connect at all, see KB-007. If the whole connection drops, not only the VPN, see KB-008.
