# New starter equipment and accounts

New starters need equipment and accounts before their first day. The manager raises the request.

## What the manager provides, at least five working days ahead

1. The new starter's full name, job title, team and start date.
2. Their manager's name and the cost centre.
3. The equipment needed: laptop, monitor, headset, phone or others, and whether they will work in the office, at home or both.
4. The systems and shared folders they need, and which existing colleague's access they should mirror.
5. Any software beyond the standard set.

## What happens

1. The service desk prepares the laptop and orders any other equipment.
2. Access Management creates the accounts and adds the requested access, with approval from the manager. This includes email, the shared drives and the applications listed.
3. The new starter's sign-in details are given to their manager, in person or through a secure channel. They are never sent by plain email.
4. On day one, the new starter signs in, sets their password (KB-001) and registers their MFA device.

## Late requests

Requests with less than five working days' notice may not be ready in time. Say so in the ticket, and the service desk will tell you what can be done by the start date.
