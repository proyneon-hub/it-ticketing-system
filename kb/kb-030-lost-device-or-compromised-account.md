# Lost or stolen device, or a suspected compromised account

Treat these as security incidents, and report them at once. A short delay can matter.

## When this applies

- A laptop, phone or security key has been lost or stolen.
- You clicked a phishing link, opened a suspicious attachment or typed your password into a page you did not trust.
- Your account shows sign-ins you did not make, or people say they received strange messages from you.
- You were asked for your password or an MFA code and gave it.

## What the user does

1. Report it right away by phone if you can, otherwise by ticket. Say what happened, when, and which devices and accounts are involved.
2. From a device you trust, change your password (KB-001). Do not use the device you think is affected.
3. Do not delete anything and do not try to fix it yourself.

## Note for the service desk

Route to the Security Team at urgent priority immediately. Do not troubleshoot, do not close the ticket, and do not tell the user the account is safe. Disabling sessions, revoking tokens and wiping a device are done by the Security Team, not the service desk.
