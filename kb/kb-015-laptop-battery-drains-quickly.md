# Laptop battery drains quickly

The battery empties in an hour or two, or the percentage jumps around.

## Steps

1. Unplug the charger and check what is using the battery: on Windows open Settings, System, Power and battery, Battery usage; on a Mac open System Settings, Battery.
2. Close or quit any app near the top of the list, especially video calls left running in the background and browser tabs playing video.
3. Lower the screen brightness and turn on the battery saver or low power mode.
4. Restart the laptop. A stuck background process can keep the processor busy.
5. Make sure the charger is the one supplied with the laptop, and that both ends are pushed in firmly.
6. Install pending system updates.

## When it is the battery itself

A battery loses capacity with age. If the laptop is more than three years old, or shows a message such as **Service battery**, raise a ticket. A technician can check the battery health and arrange a replacement. Swollen or hot batteries are a safety risk: stop using the laptop, unplug it and call the service desk straight away.
