# Enrol a Chromebook

A Chromebook must be enrolled before it can use company accounts, apps and Wi-Fi. Enrolment attaches the device to the company's management.

## Steps

1. Turn the Chromebook on and connect it to a Wi-Fi network, such as your home network or a phone hotspot.
2. Do not sign in with a personal account. On the sign-in screen, press Ctrl, Alt and E together. The screen changes to **Enterprise enrolment**.
3. Enter your work email address and password, then approve the MFA prompt.
4. Wait for enrolment to finish. The Chromebook may restart and download settings and apps. This takes a few minutes.
5. Sign in with your work account.

## If enrolment fails

- **Enrolment not allowed** or **Device is not permitted**: the device is not in the company's inventory yet. Raise a ticket with the serial number, which is on the bottom of the Chromebook.
- A Chromebook that was already used with a personal account may need to be reset to factory settings first. Ask the service desk before you do this, because it removes local files.
