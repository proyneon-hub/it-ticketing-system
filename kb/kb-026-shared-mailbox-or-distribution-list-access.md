# Access to a shared mailbox or distribution list

A shared mailbox (such as a team inbox) or a distribution list (an address that forwards to a group) is managed by its owner. Adding you needs their approval.

## What to include in your ticket

1. The exact address of the mailbox or list.
2. What you need: read mail, send as the mailbox, or just receive the list's mail.
3. The name of the owner or your manager.
4. Why you need it.

## What happens next

1. The request goes to Access Management, who ask the owner to approve it.
2. After approval, you are added. This normally takes about an hour.
3. A shared mailbox appears in Outlook by itself within about an hour. If it does not, restart Outlook, or choose File, Account Settings and add it by its address.
4. A distribution list needs nothing from you. Mail sent to the address reaches you from then on.

## If you have access but it does not work

If you were added but cannot see the mailbox after two hours, or sending as it fails, raise a ticket that says which. If Outlook is asking for a password, see KB-024.
